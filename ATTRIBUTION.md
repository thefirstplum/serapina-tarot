# Image Attribution

The tarot card images in `frontend/src/assets/cards/` are from the Rider-Waite tarot deck (also called Rider-Waite-Smith), illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith and first published in 1909. The original artwork is in the public domain because its copyright has expired.

78 cards in total:

- Major Arcana: `maj00.jpg` to `maj21.jpg` (The Fool to The World)
- Cups, Wands, Swords: `cups01.jpg` to `cups14.jpg`, `wands01.jpg` to `wands14.jpg`, `swords01.jpg` to `swords14.jpg` (Ace to King)
- Pentacles: `pents01.jpg` to `pents14.jpg` (Ace to King)

Some modern reproductions or recolored versions of the deck may still be under copyright. The images here are based on the original public domain versions.

Reference: [Wikipedia: Rider-Waite tarot deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_tarot_deck)
